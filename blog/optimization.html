In SLAM, we don't know the post at all, and as well as map. Only given thing is clues. These places might be same (feature matching), we generally refer this as Observations. Observations can be anything, like for motion model, you observe the data from wheel encoders or measure from IMU etc. These measurements consist of noise and never going to 100% accurate.

Thing like you are training a neural network, but your ground consist of random noise. And you need to eliminate that.

Assume you are driving a car, from point A to B when you observe distance from wheel odometry, it can vary everytime. So on what things we can rely on?

We need a system/model which will take care of noise from sensors and Estimate the best fitted poses such that the error is minimum.

The Observations consist of actual pose with noise, and assuming this noise to normally distributed. Which means when we take mean of observations we come down to actual pose. And secondly they can help us to determine the confidence on current measurements. As confidence will be inversely proportional to variance in observations.

Let's dive in Understanding how does noise is being formulated before we jump in estimating the poses.

What is Gaussian Distribution? What are we expecting the result to be?

Assume, you are going a place A, and on google maps it is saying you will reach at 10:00 PM. What is the gurrantee you will reach exactly at 10:00 PM?

10:00 PM is just a reference isn't. You can reach 2 minutes before or after, depending upon unknown things.

Now, ask different question of same thing, what are chances that I will reach at 10:00PM?

Since you have refence the probability at 10:00 PM has to be more. But how can we formulate this?

The answer is very simple, we need a PDF which tells at 10, the prob is max and should reduce according time uniformly. Means, reaching 2 minutes before and after should consider as same probability.

Since we need a function which should be max at given point and reduce when we go apart. We know a function which do just opposite, $x^2$. This will be minimum at 0 and increase when we move away.

Any positive power function will be symtrical function. Question : Why $x^2$ not any postive power?

multiplying with -1. Changing the direction of function.

Now, Probabilities can't be negative, taking exp of this function. $$f(x) = e^{-x^2}$$

See, beauty of taking power of constant as function. This function can't be negative anywhere, and when $x \to \pm\infty$, $y \to 0$.

Questions : why exp? why not any number?

Remember we are going to mall, This formulation can tell us about probability for reaching at exactly at 10:00 PM. Now the question is how certain will I? Is it like 90% chances or 50 chances? how will I decide this now?

Now, the question is how does the $N$ spreads? This actually will explain by adding more samples. As we don't have exact answer to problem, and we only estimating the predictions, More samples will give more confidence and will tell how much we should trust our observations.

$\sum (x - \mu)^2$ 0.553
Variance $\sigma^2 = \frac{1}{n}\sum (x - \mu)^2$ 0.092

Above, Gaussian represents as Probability Density Function (PDF), the mean will have most probability and the confidence will be inversely proportional to spread of the graph which is nothing but represents as variance.

$$confidence \propto \frac{1}{\sigma^2}$$

Updating in above formutation, $$\mathcal{N} = e^{-x^2/\sigma^2}$$

Now, since mean can't be zero all the time, updating in formula,

$$\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}$$
